Research Profile

From signals to systems

My research explores how sensing algorithms can become efficient, dependable hardware systems, with an emphasis on FPGA acceleration, high-speed signal processing and underwater acoustics.

Research directions

Connected themes spanning physical sensing, computation and embedded deployment.

01 / Architecture

FPGA-Based Processing

Hardware architectures for deterministic, low-latency processing in sensing and control systems.

02 / Methods

High-Speed Signal Processing

Efficient pipelines that preserve algorithmic value while meeting real-world throughput constraints.

03 / Application

Underwater Acoustic Sensing

Intelligent sensing and interpretation for acoustically complex underwater environments.

Research approach

A practical workflow that keeps physical constraints visible throughout development.

Sense

Start from the signal, environment and measurement constraints.

Process

Develop efficient algorithms and data paths for the target problem.

Deploy

Translate the design into verifiable hardware and integrated systems.
